Why a phone stops getting messages

An intercom message has to reach a phone that is locked, in a pocket and doing nothing. Android saves battery by limiting what apps may do in the background, and some phone makers are stricter than others. Here is what that means for Lazy Intercom and what the in-app guide asks you to check.

Why messages can go missing

Android limits background work to save battery, and the exact rules depend on the Android version and on the phone maker. An app that has to listen while the screen is off, like an intercom, is the kind of app these limits can get in the way of. A message may then arrive late or not at all, and a locked screen may not light up.

How the app listens

Lazy Intercom keeps a small listener running so that it can hear the other phones, and Android shows a notification for it. Battery limits are what can stop that listener. Each phone has its own settings, so ask every family member to run the checklist once on their own phone.

The in-app checklist

Lazy Intercom has a built-in guide. Open Settings and find the section Make sure messages arrive. It shows four checks, and every check that is not yet fine has a Fix button that opens the right Android screen.

  • Microphone. Needed to record your voice messages.
  • Notifications. Shows the incoming message.
  • Full-screen alerts. Lets a message light up the screen when the phone is locked. This check matters on Android 14 and newer.
  • Run without battery limits. Open the battery list, find Lazy Intercom and set it to Unrestricted or Do not optimise.

Test it for real

Below the checklist is a button that sends a test message to this phone. The test arrives after 10 seconds, which gives you time to lock the screen and put your headphones on. If the message does not wake the phone, the checklist shows what to change. After you change a setting, run the test again.

Other things to check

  • The phones need to be on the same home WiFi. The household list in Settings shows whether each phone is online on WiFi, online through the relay or not seen right now, and a Find devices button searches again.
  • A phone that is offline when you send does not receive the message later through the optional relay.
  • Letting an app run without battery limits can use more battery, so it is your choice.

What this guide does not cover

Phone makers use different menus and names for their battery settings, and we have not tested every brand. That is why this page gives no brand-specific steps. It follows only what the in-app guide says. If messages still do not arrive on your phone, email kalmariumservices@gmail.com with the phone model and we will look into it.

Questions, answered

Why did a message not light up the screen?

Check Full-screen alerts and Notifications in the Make sure messages arrive section of Settings. On Android 14 and newer the full-screen alert may need to be allowed.

Should I remove battery limits for every app?

No. Only for apps that must keep listening, such as an intercom. Apps without limits can use more battery, so it is your choice.

How can I test that messages will reach me?

Use the button that sends a test message to this phone in Settings. It arrives after 10 seconds, so you can lock the screen and put your headphones on first.
